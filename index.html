The Aviary Bundle

Three small, native Mac apps that fit together — each one named after a bird, each one built to do one job well.

Files

Caracara

Gather files scattered across your drives into projects — without ever moving them. Offline-aware, searchable by text and color, and everything stays on your Mac.

In daily use. Free and open source; the public release is coming.

Boards

Pirol

Kanban boards and canvases for the work in front of you. Caracara can hand a group of files straight to a Pirol project.

In development.

Money

Curlew

Budgeting and bills. Caracara can send items with an amount and a due date to Curlew as bills.

In development.

Why birds?

Because naming apps is hard, and after enough failed attempts (including a short-lived “Salad Bundle”) an aviary was the only theme that held together. The apps are separate and never touch each other's data: they trade plain files through each other's inbox folders, and nothing leaves your Mac.
